CNC Cutting

CNC Router Cutting

High-precision flat cutting for MDF, HDHMR, plywood and solid boards. Ideal for furniture components, cabinet parts, signage boards, custom shapes and architectural profiles. Our machines handle sheets up to 8×4 ft with 0.1mm accuracy.

MDF Cutting HDHMR Plywood Bulk Orders Custom Shapes
2D Carving

2D CNC Carving

Flat decorative carving for wall panels, door facades, cabinet fronts, temple boards, headboards and architectural motifs. Upload your design or choose from our design library — we bring it to life with perfect depth and detail.

Wall Panels Door Designs Mandir Work Custom Patterns
3D Carving

3D CNC Carving

Sculptural relief carving for pillars, ceilings, furniture accents, temple interiors, decorative corbels and architectural features. Our 4-axis CNC machines produce breathtaking depth and dimensional detail in wood and MDF.

Pillars Ceiling Medallions Temple Decor Relief Art
Wall Panels

Decorative Wall Panels

Textured, carved and laminated wall panels for lobby walls, feature walls, commercial spaces and luxury residences. Available in MDF, HDHMR and wood — finished with laminates, paint or natural grain.

Wooden Mouldings
