Our Story

From a Workshop
to a Legacy

Founded in 2005 by a family of craftsmen with a passion for woodwork, Laxmi Moulding began as a modest moulding workshop in Delhi. Over the years, we embraced advanced CNC technology while never losing sight of the artisanal values that define our work.

Today, we serve architects, interior designers, furniture manufacturers and large-scale commercial contractors across India — providing precision-cut components, decorative elements and complete material solutions that elevate every project.
